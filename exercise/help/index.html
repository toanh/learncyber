Getting started with the Service Desk

Knowledge base · KB-0007 · last updated 2026-09-14 · public

Welcome to the Verdant Retail Group IT Service Desk. This portal is where store, warehouse and head-office staff raise IT tickets and where support agents pick them up. This article covers signing in for the first time.

Signing in

Once your account has been provisioned, sign in from the sign-in page with your own Verdant username (usually firstname.lastname) and the password you set during onboarding.

While your account is being set up

Personal accounts can take a day or two to be issued. In the meantime, seasonal and temporary support staff share the onboarding agent account so they can start clearing the queue straight away.

Example
To sign in with the shared onboarding account, use username helpdesk.intern with the password Welcome2024!. Please switch to your personal account as soon as it is issued, and don't share this login outside the support team.

Raising a ticket

Staff without a login can still raise a ticket through the web form on the intranet home page. Support agents work the queue from the My tickets view after signing in.

Need more help?

If you're stuck, ask your team lead or reply to your onboarding email. Please don't email passwords — raise a ticket instead.
