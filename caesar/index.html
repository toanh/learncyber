How it works

Each letter on the outer ring (plaintext) lines up with a letter on the inner ring (ciphertext). The shift is how many places the inner ring has moved. To encrypt, find your letter on the outer ring and read the inner letter next to it. To decrypt, find the letter on the inner ring and read the outer letter.
